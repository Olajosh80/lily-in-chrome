# Lily in Chrome — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-17_

Lily in Chrome lets Lily, Pathvela's AI assistant, act in your browser **only
when you ask it to and only on the tabs in its own "Lily" tab group.** This
policy explains what the extension accesses, why, and where it goes.

## What the extension accesses
When you direct Lily to perform a browser task, the extension may access, for the
tab(s) Lily is driving:

- **Page content and structure** — text, links, form fields and the accessibility
  tree, so Lily can read the page and find what to click or type.
- **Tab and window information** — titles, URLs and IDs of the tabs in Lily's own
  group, so it operates on the right tab.
- **Actions you request** — the clicks, typing, scrolling and navigation Lily
  performs on your behalf.
- **Dictation audio (optional)** — when you press the voice-typing shortcut, your
  microphone input is transcribed into the focused field. Nothing is recorded or
  kept after transcription.
- **Clipboard (write only)** — to paste content when a task requires it.

The extension does **not** read tabs outside Lily's group, does not run in
background on your personal tabs, and shows a visible yellow border on any tab it
is driving.

## How it is used and where it goes
- Page content and the task context are sent to **Pathvela / Lily** (the agent)
  **solely to carry out the task you requested** — for example, to decide the next
  click or to fill a form you asked it to fill.
- Data is used only to perform your requested actions. It is **not sold, rented,
  or used for advertising or profiling.**
- The extension itself stores only local settings (e.g. humanization preferences)
  in Chrome's extension storage on your device.

## Your control
- Lily acts only on tabs in its own group and only when you ask. Close the group,
  disable the extension, or quit Lily to stop all access immediately.
- Remove the extension at any time from `chrome://extensions`.
